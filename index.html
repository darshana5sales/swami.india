The House of Swami

We build the landmarks a growing nation lives inside.

Established in 2004, Swami India International Limited was founded on a single idea: that high-quality homes and construction should be within reach. Two decades on, that idea has become Paradise Estate, Sun Beach Village, Aquaview, Barakah — and the towers now rising over Fajara and Bijilo.

We design, contract, drill, mine and manufacture in-house. That vertical control is why our timelines hold, our finishes are consistent, and our buyers deal with one accountable name from reservation to handover.

  • Wholly integrated development, contracting and materials supply
  • 99-year and 50-year registered leasehold titles
  • In-house property management and concierge after handover
