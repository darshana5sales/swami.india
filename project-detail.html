The address

Kololi puts the Senegambia strip, the beach and the bulk of the country’s restaurants and hotels within a few minutes’ walk. It is the most rentable address we build on, which is why these apartments are the ones diaspora buyers ask about first.

The homes

One and two bedroom apartments, delivered finished rather than shelled: vitrified tiling throughout, fitted kitchens, and air-conditioning provision to bedrooms and living areas. Title is a registered leasehold, completed before handover.

Payment

Reservation from a modest deposit, then a construction-linked schedule, with flexible terms for diaspora buyers. Our sales team will walk you through the whole schedule before you commit.

Amenities

  • Manned gatehouse and CCTV coverage
  • Standby generator
  • Borehole water supply
  • On-site parking
  • In-house property management after handover
