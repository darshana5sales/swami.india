Twenty-two years of building what a growing nation lives inside.

The House of Swami

Swami India International Limited was formed in 2004 under The Gambia’s Companies Act 1955, on a single idea: that high-quality homes and construction should be available at a price people can actually reach.

A privately owned, wholly integrated developer.

Two decades on, that founding idea has become Paradise Estate, Paradise View, Sun Beach Village, Barakah Estate, Forest View Apartments, Aquaview Apartments and Airport Residency — and the towers now rising over Fajara and Bijilo.

We design, contract, drill, mine and manufacture in-house. That vertical control is why our timelines hold, why our finishes stay consistent across a whole estate, and why our buyers deal with one accountable name from reservation through to handover — and long after it.

Vision & mission

What we are building towards.

Our vision

To envision, design and construct the most magnificent landmarks and edifices in Africa — contributing to the development of the region while preserving the environment we build on.

Our mission

To meet the housing needs of a growing population, set the environmental standard for construction in The Gambia, offer quality across every segment of the market, and grow strategically rather than quickly.
