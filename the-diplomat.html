The address

The Diplomat sits in the Kanifing institutional area of Fajara — embassies, banks and offices on the doorstep, the coast a few minutes west. It is the kind of location that holds its value because nothing else can be built on it.

Ground and mezzanine levels are commercial: retail, restaurants and a pharmacy, so the building serves its residents before they leave it. Above the podium the building runs eight residential floors of eleven homes each — 88 apartments, plus the penthouse level, for 92 in total. Every floor repeats the same plate, so a home numbered 103 on the first floor is the 803 on the eighth. Sizes run from the 63.10 m² one-bedroom executive to the 164.80 m² three-bedroom.

The specification

Every home is delivered finished — not shelled. Vitrified tiling throughout, fitted kitchens, air-conditioning provision to all bedrooms and living areas, and a registered leasehold title completed before handover rather than promised after it.

Payment

Reservation from a modest deposit, then a construction-linked schedule: every instalment matches something you can see standing on the site. Terms are flexible for diaspora buyers, and our sales team will walk you through the whole schedule before you commit to anything.

Amenities

  • Gymnasium and residents’ lounge
  • Kids’ play area
  • Retail, restaurants and pharmacy on the podium
